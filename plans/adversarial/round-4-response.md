# Response to adversarial round 4 | 2026-10-09

Round 4 (`round-4.md`) accepted the plans as a design for the owner to decide
on, with 0 Blocking, 9 Major, and 11 Minor findings, eight decision texts to
correct, and eight new owner questions. Every finding is answered below with
the plan and section that changed. Fixes are design changes in `plans/`;
nothing is built, per the owner's "don't actually integrate anything just
quite yet".

## What changed in one paragraph

The design's load-bearing rules now read: `update` is assembled by category,
so every Domain arm is typed on `DomainOf<Model>` and the typed helper may
return Commands; `reconcile` declares `reads`, `writes`, and a `when`
sentence, its Navigation step reads only shared fields, and it runs after
every fold step inside a refold too; a counter's `number` is stamped from a
Domain high-water mark and its `title` is a second field; an example names its
engine once in a descriptor outside the Program that every host imports and
the build copies into the terminal manifest, so `App.define` is engine-free
again; a one-shot command parses its address into pages only, so a presented
child's Actions need the presentation open on the daemon and the CLI's
two-step delete keeps its tests; Instant offers are sealed and requests
signed; Supabase rooms are joined by the owner or an invitation, guests write
only as guests, and every table has row level security; leases are written
only by the worker identity under rules, never with the admin token; Instant's
owner rules are written beside the Supabase SQL; Dictate signs in.
