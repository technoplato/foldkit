# Response to adversarial round 3 | 2026-10-09

Round 3 (`round-3.md`) found 1 Blocking, 11 Major, and 9 Minor findings and
asked six new owner questions. Every finding is answered below with the plan
and section that changed. Fixes are design changes in `plans/`; nothing is
built, per the owner's "don't actually integrate anything just quite yet".

## Verdict asked for, and what changed

Round 3 said acceptance needs R3-01 resolved and the premises of R3-02,
R3-05, R3-08, and R3-12 corrected. All five are done:

- **R3-01** is resolved by three rules in plan 02: a Domain arm receives
  `DomainOf<Model>` and the runtime applies every Domain Message to the
  Domain part on every path alike; a declared, never-logged `reconcile` step
  carries a Domain change onto a device's own fields; a Domain declaration's
  `leadsTo` is always `stay`. Plans 05, 07, 08, 01, and 01a are restated
  against those rules.
- **R3-02**: README decision 6 and plan 06 decision 1 now say what an offer
  buys with nothing saved (an earlier read-only paint, not a shorter wait)
  and offer the owner the same-actor same-build option and in-memory
  checkpoints.
- **R3-05**: `App.define` declares `defaultEngine`, `FOLDKIT_ENGINE`
  overrides it, `local` is the default only for an App that declares none,
  `Host.server` exists, and an App with `serverOnly` work declares it.
- **R3-08**: `ClosedDay` carries `closesAtMs`, the clock rule has one
  declared exception, `SetTimeZone` exists, and decision 25 is restated.
- **R3-12**: the script scores every app, each proxy cites its rubric row,
  hostless directories are unscored, the reading pass is the authority, and
  the owner is asked whether apps without `Program.make` are in.
