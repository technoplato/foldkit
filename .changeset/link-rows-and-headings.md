A list row can be a link: give it an `href`, such as a bookmark's moment `/books/a-new-earth/listen/12m03s`, and every Client paints it as a link. People can open it, copy it, or press and hold to share it like any link. A plain click still opens in-app paths in the Program. A terminal opens a link row with Enter, and OpenTUI with a click. A transcript passage can carry a `heading`, such as `Chapter 8: Religion`, painted above it.
