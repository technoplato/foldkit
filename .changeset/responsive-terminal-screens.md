Terminal UIs fit the terminal and keep up with the Program. The new `paintTerminal` paints a bound Program exactly as tall as the terminal: one line naming where it is, the body scrolled to the highlighted button or to what is current, such as the chapter playing or the line with the word being spoken, with a line saying how much is hidden above or below, then the docks and the key hints, which always show. `runProgramTui` paints with it on the terminal's own screen, restored on quit, and folds Model changes that land faster than a paint into one repaint, so a Program that changes every second never keeps a key waiting. Escape reads as plain Escape, although Node's readline reports it with Meta held, so a terminal can close a Sheet and go back again. A screen keeps its highlight by its identity, so a player whose address follows its place, such as `/books/a-new-earth/listen/12m03s`, keeps its highlight while it plays, and a Sheet opens on its current row, or the first after it that presses, such as the chapter after the one playing. Layout marks those rows with `LayoutBox.isCurrent`, and the terminal painter composes each row from the runs written on it instead of a cell map, about twelve times faster for a 650-line screen.
