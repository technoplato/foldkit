A Transcript's passages can now say more about themselves, and the HTML painter draws long Transcripts without rebuilding every word. `TranscriptPassage` adds `startMs` (where the passage starts on the clock), `isFocused` (the passage a link opened on, such as `/recording/r1/section/s7`), `isTentative` (words still being heard, drawn quieter), `text` (what a passage with no timed words says), and `sealed` (why its words are held back, `Words kept private`, drawn as a quiet placeholder and read aloud by a screen reader). `TranscriptNode` adds `passageMenu`, the menu a Program holds open on a passage, which a passage's `labelAction` asks for; the HTML painter now draws `labelAction` as a button and puts the menu by the passage's time. The HTML painter gives words no handlers of their own: one listener at the edge hears every click, right click and long press and sends `fk-transcript-press`, and a click that ends a text selection, or a right click on selected words, is left to the reader. `paintHtml` takes `{ passageSlots: createPassageSlots() }`, so a page draws again only the passages that are new values. The edge Mounts (`transcriptMount`, `passageMenuMount`, `wordMenuMount`, `itemMenuMount`, `listMount`) are exported from `foldkit/renderers/html` for tests to answer. A terminal prints a sealed passage's reason and an untimed passage's text. `OnCustomEvent` may return `undefined` to send nothing.
