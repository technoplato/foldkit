A choosing Action can take values its choices don't list. Give its `choose` an `accepts` rule, and a token that decodes and passes the rule is taken, so a seek bar can press `SeekTo:723000` for any place while `choicesOf` still offers the chapter starts. An open choice is Enabled while its Action is, and its entry says `isOpen: true`. `Catalog.within(child, { childOf, nothing })` offers a child Program's Actions from a parent that holds at most one of the child, such as the player inside a library. Each Action keeps its tag, fields, words, keys, and choices, reads the child's Model for its rule, and says `nothing` (`'nothing is in the player'`) while there is no child. `childOf` reads a parent Message back as the child's own. Two Actions may now share a key when only one is offered at a time: `Interaction.keyedEntryOf` gives `p` to Pause while playing and to Play while paused.
